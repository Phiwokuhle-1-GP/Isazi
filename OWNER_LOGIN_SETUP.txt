ISAZI HERBAL SHOP — OWNER PORTAL LOGIN SETUP
=============================================

This package has no preset password. Never share or commit your actual password.

1) On Windows, extract the ZIP and run RUN_WINDOWS.bat.
2) If installing for the first time, enter a private password of at least 12 characters twice.
3) Visit http://127.0.0.1:5000/
4) Click "Owner & team sign in", or open http://127.0.0.1:5000/login

INITIAL OWNER LOGIN
Username: owner
Password: the private password you chose on first launch
Owner dashboard: http://127.0.0.1:5000/owner

TO CHANGE YOUR PASSWORD
Sign in > Security > Change password. After the change, sign in again.

FORGOT PASSWORD — LOCAL ADMINISTRATOR RESET
Open PowerShell in the project folder, stop the server, then run:
  .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe app.py reset-password --username owner

EXISTING INSTALLATIONS
If you already created a login with the older Agile Studio, your username may be "admin".
Your existing admin account still signs in and has owner access. Do not delete instance/isazi.sqlite3.
Back up the existing instance/ folder before replacing files.

SECURITY
- Only the under-construction landing page is public.
- The owner dashboard, editable project plan and history require login.
- Customer checkout, payment processing and actual product/order records do not exist yet.
- Do not expose the Flask development server to the internet.
